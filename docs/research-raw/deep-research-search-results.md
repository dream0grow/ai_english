## 검색 축

### Transforming language education: A systematic review of AI-powered chatbots for EFL speaking practice (Computers & Education: AI)
- URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666920X24000316
- 관련도: high
- 요약: Systematic review directly on-topic: AI chatbots for English speaking practice, synthesizing evidence on gains in fluency, pronunciation, grammatical accuracy, vocabulary and confidence, plus reduced speaking anxiety. Strongest single academic anchor for the report's 'effectiveness' ranking axis.

### Duolingo Efficacy Studies (official research hub)
- URL: https://www.duolingo.com/efficacy/studies
- 관련도: high
- 요약: Duolingo's own efficacy research portal, including studies claiming ~half of Spanish/French course learners reach A2+ CEFR speaking. Essential for verifying (and caveating — vendor-funded, beginner-level, pronunciation weakest) Duolingo/Duolingo Max CEFR-improvement claims cited in marketing.

### English major students' satisfaction with ELSA Speak in English pronunciation courses (peer-reviewed, PMC)
- URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11717304/
- 관련도: high
- 요약: Peer-reviewed study of 205 students using ELSA Speak: high satisfaction and improved engagement/pronunciation skills. One of the few app-specific academic evaluations of ELSA — key evidence, but note it measures satisfaction more than objective CEFR gains.

### AI-driven chatbots in second language education: A systematic review of their efficacy and pedagogical implications
- URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2215039025000086
- 관련도: high
- 요약: 2020–2024 systematic review finding substantial positive effects of AI chatbots on L2 proficiency, motivation, and anxiety reduction, grounded in SLA theory (Krashen's Input Hypothesis, Long's Interaction Hypothesis) — useful for the report's theoretical justification of conversational AI practice, including corrective-feedback/recast pedagogy.

### AI-powered conversation bots in enhancing L2 speaking skills and reducing speaking anxiety: a mixed methods study (Nature Humanities & Social Sciences Communications)
- URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-05550-z
- 관련도: medium
- 요약: High-quality mixed-methods empirical study showing AI conversation bots improve L2 speaking and lower speaking anxiety — supports a family-app selling point (low-pressure practice for a shy child/adult) with journal-grade evidence, though not app-brand-specific.

### Comparing the effectiveness of Duolingo, classroom, and classroom+Duolingo on beginner French development (Studies in Second Language Acquisition)
- URL: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/studies-in-second-language-acquisition/article/comparing-the-effectiveness-of-duolingo-classroom-instruction-and-classroom-duolingo-instruction-conditions-on-beginnerlevel-french-language-development/68C0E7E296669798089C84CDC7F3BB9E
- 관련도: medium
- 요약: Independent (non-vendor) controlled comparison of Duolingo vs. classroom instruction in a top SLA journal — the credibility counterweight to Duolingo's in-house efficacy claims when scoring price-vs-effectiveness.
